Our Story

Built in classrooms. Proven in institutions. Ready to scale.

Pupil Primo was created from a simple belief: the strongest education businesses are built when academic purpose, professional management and patient capital work together.

Our journey has been shaped by years of hands-on experience in building and operating schools and colleges — understanding what parents expect, what students need, what teachers require to succeed, and what makes an institution sustainable for the long term.

That experience now comes together under Pupil Primo Edu Ventures — a platform designed to build new institutions, strengthen existing ones, invest alongside partners and create education networks that can grow without losing quality.

From operating experience to an education platform.

Our foundation is not theory. It comes from running education institutions, building teams, growing admissions, strengthening academics and creating trusted brands. Pupil Primo takes that operating DNA and combines it with capital, technology and disciplined execution to create the next generation of education institutions.
